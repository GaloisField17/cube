The Yellow Corners

At the end of this step, we will have completed the corners.

We will use one algorithm repeatedly to solve the corners. Depending on the arrangement of the yellow corners, we will orient the cube correctly before each application of the algorithm.

Two Corners With Same Facelet on One Side

If one side has 2 corners with same facelet, we rotate the Up face to match the corners with the center of their facelets and make that face our Back face.

In short, we orient our cube as one of the following examples:

Two orange facelets
Two blue facelets
Two red facelets
Two green facelets

Each Side With Different Facelet

If we can't have one of the orientations above, we rotate the Up face to match one corner then make that face our Back face.

In short, we orient our cube so it looks like one of the following:

Algorithm: R' F R' B2 R F' R' B2 R2

After orienting the corners as described above, we will apply the following algorithm:

R' F R' B2 R F' R' B2 R2

We then rotate the Up face to bring the corners on the appropiate faces. If we end up with two facelets of the same colors on each side, we are done. Otherwise, we rotate the Up face as described above and apply the algorithm again.
