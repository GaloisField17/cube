The Yellow Cross

In this step, we will form the yellow cross on the top face by applying a single algorithm up to three times.

After each application, depending on the orientation of the edges on the Up face, we need to orient the cube correctly before applying the algorithm again.

Orienting the Up Face

If there are no yellow edges facing up, we can jump straight into the algorithm. If there are two yellow edges, we rotate the cube or the Up face so that
  • a yellow edge is on the Left face, and
  • a non-yellow edge is on the front face.
The orientation of corners doesn't matter. In short, we want our cube to be in one of the orientations below.
No yellow edges
Two yellow edges,
90-degree angle
Two yellow edges,
180-degree angle

There are no other possible orientations of the Up face due to a legal 3×3 cube having an even parity. [1] [2]

Algorithm: F U R   U' R' F'

After orienting the cube as described above, we can apply the algorithm. Remember the orientation of the cube does not matter.

We will apply the following algorithm up to three times, making sure the cube is oriented correctly before applying the algorithm again.

F U R   U' R' F'.

We can remember the yellow cross algorithm using the mnemonic: FUR says U' R' F!
