The Second Layer

↑ Introduction White Cross White Corners Second Layer Yellow Cross Yellow Face Yellow Corners Yellow Edges

At the end of this step, we will have the bottom two layers solved.

We start by rotating the cube so the yellow center is on the Up face.

Depending on the position of the edges on the Up face, we will set up our moves as follows:

An Edge on the Up Face Has No Yellow

If there is an edge with no yellow on it, we first align it with its corresponding center by rotating the Up face. We then rotate the cube so that the edge is on the Front face.

The color of its Up-face facelet determines whether the edge will be inserted into the Right face or the Left face.

In this case, the edge is red, and the red center is on the Right face. Thus, the edge will be inserted into the Right face.

We therefore use the Right algorithm:

U R U' R'   U' F' U F.

Otherwise, we use the Left algorithm:

U' L' U L   U F U' F'.

All Edges on the Up Face Have Yellow

If every edge on the Up face has yellow on it, we use either the Right or Left algorithm, depending on the position of the unsolved edge, to bring it onto the Up face.

In this case, the unsolved edge is on the Right face. Therefore, we use the Right algorithm.

Note that since every edge on the Up face contains yellow, we do not need to perform the first U move in the Right algorithm. Our goal is simply to bring the unsolved edge onto the Up face, so it does not matter which edge on the Up face is displaced.

We then match the edge with its corresponding center and rotate the cube so that the matched edge is on the Front face. Finally, we use the Right or Left algorithm as above.
