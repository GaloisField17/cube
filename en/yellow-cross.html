The Yellow Cross

↑ Introduction White Cross White Corners Second Layer Yellow Cross Yellow Face Yellow Corners Yellow Edges

In this step, we will form the yellow cross on the Up face by applying a single algorithm up to three times.

After each application, depending on the orientation of the edges on the Up face, we need to orient the cube correctly before applying the algorithm again.

Orienting the Up Face

If there are no yellow edges facing up, we can jump straight into the algorithm. If there are two yellow edges, we rotate the cube or the Up face so that:

  • a yellow edge is on the Left face, and
  • a non-yellow edge is on the Front face.

The orientation of the corners does not matter. In short, we want our cube to be in one of the orientations below.

No yellow edges
Two yellow edges,
90-degree angle
Two yellow edges,
180-degree angle

There are no other possible orientations of the Up face because a legal 3×3 cube has even parity. [1] [2]

Algorithm: F U R   U' R' F'

After orienting the cube as described above, we can apply the algorithm. Remember, the orientation of the corners doesn't matter.

We may need to apply the algorithm up to three times, reorienting the cube between applications as necessary.

F U R   U' R' F'.

We can remember the yellow cross algorithm with the mnemonic: FUR says U' R' F!
