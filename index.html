Beginner's Method for Solving the 3×3 Rubik's Cube

This tutorial is my attempt to share the beginner’s method for solving the 3×3 cube using simple techniques and algorithms. It assumes no prior experience and includes interactive cube animations, powered by Roofpig, as well as illustrations for moves and rotations.

Together, we will solve the cube in seven steps:

  1. The White Cross
  2. The White Corners
  3. The Second Layer
  4. The Yellow Cross
  5. The Yellow Face
  6. The Yellow Corners
  7. The Yellow Edges

Before we start solving the cube, we will first go over some basic notation and terminology that will be used throughout the tutorial.

Notation

A standard 3×3 cube has 3 layers and 6 faces. A solved cube is a state in which each face is a solid color: red, green, orange, blue, white, or yellow.

The cube is made up of 26 smaller visible cube pieces, which can be classified as center pieces, edge pieces, or corner pieces. Each type of piece, as well as the layers, faces, and moves, has a straightforward and unambiguous name.

Layers

Layers are the horizontal levels of the cube. They are called the bottom layer, the middle layer, and the top layer. The pieces that make up each layer depend on the orientation of the cube.

Pieces

The cube consists of 27 smaller pieces. These pieces are also referred to as cubies in cubing communities. In this tutorial, we'll simply refer to them as pieces. Of the 27 pieces, 26 are visible. Each visible piece can be a center piece, a corner piece, or an edge piece. Each piece is characterized by its type and colors.

The remaining component is the core of the cube, which holds the other pieces together and allows them to rotate.

A piece's facelet is a single colored square on its face. Corner pieces have three facelets, edge pieces have two, and center pieces have one.

Faces

A face is the set of pieces covering one whole side of the cube. We name the faces according to their position relative to us: Front (F), Back (B), Right (R), Left (L), Up (U), and Down (D). Each face has nine pieces covering its 3×3 area: four corner pieces, four edge pieces, and one center piece. In the illustrations below, the corresponding face is highlighted in color.
