Beginner's Method for Solving the 3x3 Rubik's Cube

Introduction

To solve the cube, we will divide our journey in to 6 steps.

  1. The White Cross
  2. The White Corners
  3. The Second Layer
  4. The Yellow Cross
  5. The Yellow Corners
    1. Solving The Yellow Face
    2. Solving The Yellow Corners
  6. The Yellow Edges

The White Cross

We want to form a white cross where white edges match with the center pieces.

Start by rotating the white center Up side.

Example: Edge Matches With Center

Bring the white edge up by rotating the Right side.

Example: Missing White Edge Would Disrupt Solved Cross Pieces

Place the missing white edge on the Middle layer by rotating Face side.

To preserve the solved white pieces, rotate the Up side so the unsolved (gray) pieces line with the missing white edge.

Then, bring the missing white edge up by rotating Right side.

Finally, rotate Up side to match edges with centers.

The White Corners

We want solve the white corners, which will complete the layer with the white center.

Example: White Corner's White Side Is Not Facing Up Side or Down Side

If an unmatched white corner is at the bottom layer with its white side not facing up side or down side,

first match its neighbour with its center by rotating Down side,

then, rotate the cube so the edge's white color is on the Face side.

Use R F' R' F or L' F L F' to bring the corner Up side.

If it is at the top layer with its white side not facing up side or down side,

bring it down by F' D' F or F D F',

then, match the edge with its center piece and rotate the cube so the white color of the edge is on the Face side,

then use R F' R' F or L' F L F' as above.

Example: White Corner's White Side Is Facing Up Side or Down Side

If it is facing Up side, similarly as previous step,

bring it down to the bottom layer by using F D F'.

Then use R' F' R F or L F L' F' to bring it on the Up side.

If it's facing Down side, bring it down up to the bottom layer by using F D' F'.

Then use R' F' R F or L F L' F' to bring it on the Up side.

The Second Layer

We want finish solving the second layer.

Rotate the yellow center Up side.

Example: There Is An Edge In Up Side With No Yellow On It

If there is an edge with no yellow in it, match the edge with its center by rotating Up side and rotate the cube so this edge is on the Face side.

Its color on the Up side determines which side it will go in to.

In this case, it's red, and the red center is on the Right side. Thus, the edge will go in the Right side.

So, use the Right Algorithm as follows:

U R U' R'   U' F' U F.

Otherwise, use the Left Algorithm.

U' L' U L   U F U' F'.

Example: Every Edge In Up Side With Has Yellow On It

If every edge on the Up side has yellow on it, use either the Right, or the Left algorithm on the side of an unsolved edge to bring it on the Up side.
